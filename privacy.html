Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Progressive Web App Mijn PWA App.

1. What data do we collect?

Mijn PWA App is designed with privacy in mind. The app works largely offline and by default collects no personal data. Depending on your usage, the following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • Preferences and settings (local browser storage)
  • Data you enter that is required for the app to work

This data does not leave your device and is not sent to any server.

2. Cookies

This app does not place tracking cookies itself. To work as a PWA it uses a service worker and your browser cache. This is not a cookie and contains no personal data.

3. Hosting

The app is hosted on a secure web server. The hosting provider may log technical data (such as IP address) for security and analysis purposes. We do not access or process this data.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

Since we do not process personal data, there is no data to request, modify or delete. If you have any questions about data that could be linked to you, you can contact us.

5. Contact

You can contact us via the contact form on the website.

6. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the app changes. The most recent version is always available on this page.
