📖 User Guide — Mijn PWA App

Everything you need to know to install and use MijnApp.

1. What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a website that you can install as a real app on your phone, tablet or computer. Benefits:

  • Works offline after the first load
  • Appears with an icon on your home screen or desktop
  • Starts faster than a regular website
  • No app store required

2. Install on your device

📱 Android (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open the website in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Choose "Install app" or "Add to home screen".
  4. Confirm. The icon is now on your home screen.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the website in Safari (not Chrome — that does not work on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm with "Add".

💻 Windows / macOS / Linux (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open the website in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (small screen with arrow).
  3. Or: menu → "Install app".
  4. The app opens in its own window without browser bars.

3. Offline use

After the first load, the app is stored by a service worker. Afterwards, the app works without an internet connection. New updates are fetched automatically when you are online again.

💡 Tip: open the app once while online, after that it works offline.

4. Updating

When a new version is available:

  1. Open the app while online.
  2. Close the app completely (not just minimise).
  3. Open it again. The new version is loaded.

Not working? Restart your device or clear the browser cache.

5. Uninstall

  • Android: press and hold the icon → "Uninstall".
  • iOS: press and hold the icon → "Remove app".
  • Desktop: right-click the icon → "Uninstall".

6. Troubleshooting

The app does not install

  • Check that you access the site via HTTPS (not HTTP).
  • On iOS: use Safari, not Chrome or Firefox.
  • On Android: use Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet.

The app does not work offline

  • Open the app once while online.
  • Check that the connection works correctly.

Old version keeps showing

  • Close the app completely and reopen.
  • Clear the cache: settings → clear browsing data.
